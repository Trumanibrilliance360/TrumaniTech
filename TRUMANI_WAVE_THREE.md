# ⚡ WAVE THREE — System Depth  

The depth of a system can often be gauged by its ability to adapt and respond to the challenges it encounters. This is the essence of Wave Three.  

## Key Concepts  
1. **Resilience** - The system must be resilient to external pressures and able to recover from setbacks.  
2. **Adaptability** - As new challenges arise, the capacity to adapt is crucial for survival and success.  
3. **Innovative Capacity** - Being deeply innovative allows for transformation and growth in both stable and turbulent times.  

## Components of System Depth  
- **Layered Architecture** - A deep system has a multi-layered architecture that allows for modular updates and scalability.  
- **Dynamic Interaction** - The ability for components to interact dynamically ensures responsiveness to change.  
- **Feedback Loops** - Effective feedback loops ensure the system learns from past interactions and continues to improve.  

## Importance of Wave Three  
The transition into Wave Three signifies a pivotal moment for any organization. It requires embracing a mindset geared toward not just surviving, but thriving amid change.  

> "The strongest systems are those that can evolve without losing their core values."
